# Product challenge — TASK-016

1. **Filtering and rejection have an unclear precedence.** US2 scenario 1 and FR-006 require excluding matches outside the interval; the edge cases exclude non-completed matches. FR-008 simultaneously prohibits silently dropping invalid records. A source portion containing a valid requested match and a malformed out-of-range or scheduled match has no explicit oracle. Rejecting every raw record makes narrow requests fail on irrelevant data; filtering before checking scope can hide corrupt facts. **Recommendation:** document the validation sequence in the plan: validate enough scope/status/kickoff information to determine eligibility, exclude demonstrably out-of-scope records, then validate retained facts and their reference closure. An undecidable eligibility field must produce invalid-response. Add mixed-response fixtures, including malformed eligible records, to prove no selective repair occurs.

2. **“Equivalent football facts” needs an identity-aware test oracle.** US1 scenario 1, FR-005 and SC-001 combine result-local opaque references, different source identifiers and unordered collections. Literal equality can reject equivalent results; comparison by display name can accept an accidental merge of same-name players. **Recommendation:** fixtures should declare an explicit source-independent correspondence for expected entities, and shared checks should compare relationships and values under a reference bijection while checking provenance separately. Keep this correspondence in test support. Do not promote fixture matching into production reconciliation. Include a same-name pair whose swapped team or performance relationship must fail comparison.

3. **The error envelope needs adversarial evidence, not just category coverage.** FR-009 permits explanations and affected fields/references; FR-012 prohibits secret-bearing URLs and raw exception text. FR-013 requires all categories but does not explicitly exercise hostile values in those diagnostic slots. Passing ordinary category fixtures would not establish that adapter diagnostics cannot echo credentials. **Recommendation:** generate explanations from controlled templates and expose only validated diagnostic identifiers. Test sentinel credentials embedded in exceptions, URLs, unknown request-field names and source references across complete caller-visible outcomes. Retaining opaque identifiers verbatim is acceptable only after the stated provenance safety rule is satisfied.

4. **Contract success is insufficient evidence of CP2 provider feasibility.** FR-007 allows every optional count to be unknown, FR-006 permits completed matches without performances, and the assumptions explicitly make no vendor-capability guarantee. Therefore passing synthetic checks cannot demonstrate that a selected source supplies usable valuation inputs for the five leagues in PRODUCT.md. **Recommendation:** record this downstream risk for TASK-017/TASK-022: assess operation, league and metric coverage, and distinguish supported empty results from unavailable coverage before claiming CP2 readiness. Keep live-provider research and missing-input valuation policy outside TASK-016; requiring them here would expand scope.
