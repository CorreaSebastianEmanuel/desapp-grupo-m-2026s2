# Product challenge — TASK-017

1. **Success fixtures may require evidence the source does not supply.** FR-002/005 require verified season scope across portions. The documented [Team response](https://docs.football-data.org/general/v4/team.html) has running competitions and `lastUpdated`, but no squad-specific season discriminator. **Recommendation:** architecture must identify actual fields and documented semantics establishing scope for each chosen endpoint; do not add invented season fields to successful fixtures. Compare a complete competition-team response with separate team retrievals bracketed by discovery checks. Bracketing can detect a season rollover, but cannot establish an atomic roster snapshot; document that limitation and test changed discovery/contradictory portions rather than treating retrieval time as season evidence.

2. **Unique affiliation can still be stale.** [Source policies](https://docs.football-data.org/general/v4/policies.html) describe squads as players appearing in a team's roster during the season. Consequently, a transferred player appearing only in the former team's returned squad can pass duplicate checks without establishing present membership. FR-003's “unambiguous” relationship and the contract's current affiliation need a documented evidentiary interpretation. **Recommendation:** demonstrate which source evidence justifies that relationship and add a one-sided stale-transfer fixture, beyond the cross-team duplicate case already identified in `product.md`. Reject insufficient evidence; choosing the first team or reconstructing transfers would introduce unsupported business behavior.

3. **Complete retrieval may exceed the accessible quota.** Policies document 10 requests/minute for free access. A discovery/list/per-team strategy could require more requests than that for one league, while FR-012 forbids waiting/retries and imposes a shared five-second default. **Recommendation:** record worst-case request counts and latency assumptions, and test quota exhaustion partway through a realistic league-sized exchange. Prefer a documented complete bulk response when available; otherwise explain per-team retrieval's access requirements without making a subscription purchase part of acceptance. Tiny success fixtures do not establish operational usability.

4. **Approved hostname alone does not prove credential containment.** FR-009/015 must survive transport behavior, not just candidate sanitization. **Recommendation:** exercise transport-generated errors/diagnostics, cross-origin and same-origin redirects, and hostile source IDs embedded in request paths. Compare a fixed endpoint builder with unrestricted URL composition; recommend fixed HTTPS origin/port and encoded path segments. Verify no redirect request or diagnostic emission occurs before sanitization, and observe owned connection cleanup independently of worker death.

5. **Checkpoint evidence could overstate delivered capability.** CP2 requires valuation strategies and dated quotes, while FR-006 deliberately provides no performance inputs. **Recommendation:** architecture's coverage evidence must distinguish adapter compatibility from usable valuation ingestion. Offline catalog success and explicit performance refusal cannot demonstrate the statistics-to-quote path. Carry this dependency risk into checkpoint planning; a new source, inferred minutes, persistence or live-account requirement would exceed FR-017.
