# Product challenge — TASK-055

1. **Synthetic verification can prove the wrong adapter.** FR-013/014 require source-shaped examples, but the assumptions permit synthetic facts FotMob may never publish. Two repeatable runs and equivalence with a fixture provider can therefore pass without demonstrating a usable FotMob translation. Recommend that the architect distinguish evidenced source fields from hypothetical fields in the mapping and fixture inventory. Exercise known source omissions as failures; do not add invented FotMob fields solely to obtain valid performances. Synthetic examples remain useful for contract behavior, but must not support source-coverage claims. A broader hypothetical source adapter is an alternative requiring a separate scope decision.

2. **Completeness needs a falsifiable witness.** FR-005/006 reject incomplete discovery and rosters, but do not identify how completeness will be established. TASK-016 explicitly allows completed matches without performances; indiscriminately rejecting empty appearances would narrow that contract, while accepting every empty array could hide a failed detail fetch. Recommend a source-specific completeness rule for discovery, pagination and each required detail, with independent examples distinguishing a published empty collection from missing, truncated or undecidable content. Include a match with legitimately no supplied performances. Where the source offers no reliable witness, preserve the specified failure or unsupported assessment rather than infer completeness from HTTP success or expected team counts.

3. **Access limits cross individual request boundaries.** FR-002/003 require applicable permission and documented cadence, volume and concurrency limits; FR-010 assigns later workers responsibility for respecting limits, while the assumptions also require enforcement in the plan. Multiple portions or simultaneous callers could exceed permission despite individually valid assessments. Recommend explicit ownership of aggregate limits, assessment expiry/withdrawal during retrieval, and redirected destinations. Test that unapproved destinations and subsequent work after assessment invalidation cannot initiate retrieval. Either enforce approved limits at the shared transport boundary or keep activation blocked until caller coordination can establish compliance; do not treat a scheduler setting as sufficient evidence.

4. **Offline completion must not conceal a CP2 dependency gap.** FR-015 allows offline-only delivery, but docs/CP2_ARCHITECTURE.md places this task upstream of ingestion and valuation. The spec records 62 of 216 sampled performances missing match positions; TASK-021 feedback forbids treating usualPosition as proven frequency. Recommend explicit delivery evidence that distinguishes adapter completion from usable live statistics coverage and names the remaining access/position blockers. Preserve fixture-labelled CP2 demonstration as the documented alternative. Do not resolve the substitute policy here or interpret this task's PASS as production ingestion readiness.
