# Independent product challenge — TASK-022

1. **Self-consistent replay tampering can evade arithmetic validation.** FR-011 permits embedded facts or immutable references; FR-012 requires validation of original fact identity/values. Story 4.7 exercises disagreements, but changing a raw count together with its total, feature and score leaves no internal disagreement. That could manufacture a reproducible-looking result under a real performance identity. Recommendation: designate authoritative evidence and test this coordinated alteration, including historical position and kickoff. Prefer resolving exact original IDs against immutable Statistics facts (ADR-0010), comparing any supplied snapshots before recomputation. A trusted retained snapshot is an alternative when original facts cannot be recovered, but merely recomputing a caller-supplied snapshot or checking its caller-supplied digest does not establish originality. Durable snapshot storage remains TASK-026; TASK-022 must define the trust boundary and explicit unavailable-evidence failure.

2. **The scorer contract needs enforceable ownership of requirements.** FR-002 supplies metric-scope requirements separately from scoring configuration; the Strategy Definition entity also owns requirements. FR-014 prohibits minute-based contributions and nondeterministic dependencies, while FR-008 exposes raw minutes and metrics as evidence. An arbitrary callback can ignore these restrictions, or consume a configuration whose weighted metrics differ from the prepared requirements. Recommendation: architecture should bind strategy identity/version, declared requirements and effective configuration to one validated trusted scorer definition; incompatible requests must fail without scoring. Exercise a configuration requiring an undeclared metric and a scorer returning an ordinary failure. Trusted reviewed modules are sufficient; a general callback sandbox or runtime proof of determinism would expand scope. If callbacks are retained for illustrative tests, document their conformance obligations and keep them outside any untrusted execution boundary.

3. **Mixed present/absent required scopes lack a direct acceptance example.** Story 3.7 covers only the case where no required scope applies. FR-005 deliberately leaves absent groups absent, but a request containing defender tackles and goalkeeper saves with only defender performances also needs an oracle. Recommendation: test that preparation retains the defender feature, omits goalkeeper features, and never invents saves or a goalkeeper contribution. A scorer requiring the absent feature must produce an ordinary no-score failure rather than crash or silently use zero. A strategy explicitly combining only populated groups may instead succeed under its declared rules; production combination policy belongs to TASK-024. Do not make preparation globally fail for every absent declared group or choose combination weights here.
