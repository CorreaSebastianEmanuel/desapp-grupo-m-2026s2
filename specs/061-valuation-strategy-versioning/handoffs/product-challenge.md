# Product challenge — TASK-025

- **Concurrency guarantee has an ABA boundary.** FR-008 compares the expected configuration identity, while US2.3 expressly permits restoring A after B. A delayed request expecting A can therefore succeed after A→B→A; identity comparison cannot detect intervening changes. Recommend value-based comparison as specified, a controlled cycle regression, and describing the competing-change guarantee per uninterrupted selection state. An activation revision would detect intervening changes but would introduce a stronger rejection policy and an additional caller token; do not add it silently or claim the current contract provides that guarantee.

- **Numeric equality is insufficient persistence evidence.** FR-005 requires canonical evidence semantics, and `lib/football_market/valuation/evidence.ex` encodes integer 1 differently from Decimal 1 while canonicalizing equivalent Decimals. A numeric column or JSON number alone can lose this distinction even when scores remain equal. Recommend a typed exact codec and restart assertions comparing inherited canonical evidence, including integer/Decimal pairs, equivalent Decimal encodings, negative zero, large coefficients and extreme exponents under different Decimal contexts. Fixed-scale numeric storage simplifies queries but risks rounding or ceilings; a typed coefficient/exponent representation preserves the required semantics. Do not replace the common evidence format or choose executable modules from persisted names.

- **An absent mutation API does not establish immutability.** US4.2/FR-006 protect even unused versions; FR-009 protects selection history. Tests that merely omit update/delete functions cannot demonstrate these guarantees against persistence writes or cascades. Recommend defining the enforcement boundary for ordinary application database access and exercising rejected updates/deletes, including creation metadata, unused rules and selection-change records. Database enforcement offers protection across application write paths; context-only checks depend on every writer cooperating. Retain the trusted internal management boundary in FR-001; adding trader-accessible administration would require a separate permissions decision.

- **Restart reproduction can overrun the task boundary.** US4 and SC-004 require replay after restart, but FR-017 assigns durable calculation-descriptor retention to TASK-026. Recommend retaining the complete descriptor in the acceptance harness across restart and testing production lookup of its configuration identity, without introducing quote/history storage here. `docs/PRODUCT.md` requires each quote's version/effective date and reproducibility; `docs/CHECKPOINTS.md` retains dated quotes and active-strategy ranking. Passing TASK-025 establishes a prerequisite, not those checkpoint outcomes. Carry the version/descriptor integrity integration and intentional production calibration into downstream guidance; successful fixture scores alone cannot establish market readiness.
