# Product Challenge — TASK-028

1. **Interrupted execution has two materially different outcomes.** SC-003 requires no effects in 100% of interrupted cases, but Story 2 scenario 3 only covers failure before completion. An interruption after database commit but before the caller observes success cannot erase append-only issuance. **Recommendation:** architecture and QA should distinguish pre-commit rollback from lost acknowledgement after commit. Inject both failures: the former permits first issuance on retry; the latter returns the retained issuance without another allocation. Do not implement compensating deletion to satisfy the broader wording. Durable completion and FR-008 resolve this distinction without a new product choice.

2. **A conserved total alone does not prove valid inventory.** FR-011 names total holdings other than 100 and missing issuance facts, but does not explicitly test contradictory facts or offsetting invalid holdings. Quantities of 120 and -20 sum to 100; a fully populated issuance could reference another player's quote or a quote inconsistent with its recorded one-credit value. These states undermine SC-005 despite passing presence and aggregate checks. **Recommendation:** interpret FR-011's “including” as non-exhaustive. Define consistency checks for nonnegative whole holdings, valid owner/player identities, canonical issuance quantity/value, and agreement with the associated original quote and recipient. Use adversarial fixtures; reject rather than repair. Zero holdings for the original recipient after trading remain valid.

3. **The concurrency evidence must include adjacent financial writes.** Story 2 tests competing initialization and sequential later trading/quotes; it does not exercise a retry while ownership moves, or first issuance while a quote is inserted. Reading holdings across separate snapshots can falsely report corruption during an otherwise atomic transfer. A precondition checked before a competing quote commits can admit the previously forbidden quote history (FR-011). **Recommendation:** specify a coherent transactional validation boundary and a shared coordination contract for future trading and valuation writers. Verify these races using controlled database fixtures, without implementing those features here. A player coordination lock and serializable validation are credible alternatives; choose based on existing persistence conventions and retry behavior.

4. **CP2 demonstration ordering is an integration dependency.** The unquoted-player prerequisite (FR-011 and Assumptions) means a catalog player valued before issuance cannot subsequently enter the market through this feature. Catalog-wide automation and reconciliation are excluded, so this task cannot promise every catalog player becomes tradable. **Recommendation:** document explicit initialization-before-performance-valuation ordering for the CP2 demo and downstream integration. Demonstrate issuance with a fresh eligible player and rejection with preexisting quote history. Preserve the rejection rule; do not silently add backfill, ingestion hooks, or a second quote store to bypass it.
