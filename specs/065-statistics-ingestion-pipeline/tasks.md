## Implementation strategy

First establish independent expected fixtures and red contract/constraint tests, then extend boundaries/migrations without changing accepted facts. Build US1 with known direct positions and validate its four scenarios. Add US2 failure/replay/restart cases and then US3 frozen-history/unknown/lineage cases. Do not publish an intermediate MVP as complete: all three P1 stories, full final manifest execution and independent PASS gates are required. Targeted red/green runs during development are useful; final audited profiles are the acceptance checks and must not be repeated by a redundant CP1 report.
